# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains the shared process documentation for planning and delivering OctoAcme projects. The documents provide a common reference for project teams, stakeholders, and new team members.

## Project management processes summary

OctoAcme uses a lifecycle-based, iterative project management process:

1. **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define measurable outcomes, identify stakeholders, assess initial risks, and decide whether the work should proceed to planning.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved initiatives into actionable plans by prioritizing and estimating backlog work, defining acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, mapping milestones and releases, and managing dependencies.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver in small increments using a project board, regular standups, delivery syncs, demos, pull request reviews, automated quality checks, and progress metrics. Blockers are escalated from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as appropriate.
4. **Risk management and communication:** Maintain a risk register, monitor risks and dependencies, provide regular stakeholder updates, and use defined escalation paths for delivery and security concerns.
5. **Release and deployment:** Verify acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, rollback plans, smoke tests, deployment readiness, and post-deployment outcomes before and after production releases.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture what went well, identify improvements, assign action items, and measure their impact.
7. **Roles and personas:** Use clear responsibilities for Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders to support ownership and alignment throughout the lifecycle.

The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. Teams use artifacts such as the project one-pager, roadmap, backlog, acceptance criteria, Definition of Done, risk register, release plan, and retrospective action items to maintain alignment and institutional knowledge.
