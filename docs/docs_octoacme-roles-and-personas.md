## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, validation, communication, and execution of software releases. They help ensure changes are ready for production, risks are understood, and stakeholder communication is aligned.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, rollouts, and post-release verification
- Work with engineering, QA, and PMs to confirm release readiness
- Draft release notes and stakeholder communication updates
- Prepare rollback or mitigation plans for high-risk changes
- Track release health and escalate issues quickly when production impact is possible

### Goals
- Minimize release risk and disruption
- Improve predictability and clarity of deployments
- Support smooth handoffs from development to production

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and launch check-ins
- Deployment coordination with engineering and operations
- Stakeholder notifications and post-release summaries

## QA / Test Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Test Leads define and govern the quality strategy for a project. They verify that features meet acceptance criteria, support regression coverage, and help reduce avoidable defects in production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, validation plans, and key regression areas
- Partner with developers and product leads on acceptance criteria
- Prioritize defects and coordinate triage with delivery teams
- Review quality metrics and identify gaps in testing coverage
- Support release decisions with evidence from validation results

### Goals
- Deliver a reliable product experience
- Reduce defect escape and release risk
- Create a shared understanding of quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release check-ins
- Defect triage and risk discussions
- Acceptance review with product and engineering stakeholders

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Partners help ensure projects meet required controls, risk standards, and secure delivery practices. They act as a safeguard for sensitive data, policy needs, and operational risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, workflows, and changes for security and compliance concerns
- Identify risks, controls, and remediation needs early in the lifecycle
- Support secure-by-design decisions and threat/risk reviews
- Validate that dependencies, access models, and data handling meet standards
- Coordinate escalation when a security issue or policy violation is identified

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Support safe delivery without unnecessary friction
- Ensure governance requirements are built into project execution

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings, architecture discussions, and risk assessments
- Security-related escalation updates and remediation tracking
- Compliance checkpoints during planning and release phases

---

## Architecture / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Architecture / Technical Leads guide technical direction and design quality across a project or initiative. They help teams make sound decisions about system structure, integration points, and technical trade-offs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain technical direction for key systems and integrations
- Review design decisions for maintainability, scalability, and risk
- Help resolve cross-team technical dependencies and architectural conflicts
- Guide engineering decisions that affect platform health, technical debt, and reusability
- Support technical risk identification and mitigation planning

### Goals
- Keep the solution aligned to long-term architecture goals
- Reduce avoidable technical complexity and integration risk
- Support engineering teams with clear technical guardrails

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions and architecture decision discussions
- Technical dependency and integration planning
- Escalation support when execution decisions create architectural risk

---

## Customer Success / Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success / Enablement Leads represent the customer-facing view of the product or service. They ensure a project is not only delivered, but also usable, adoptable, and supported after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Translate user or customer feedback into product and rollout insights
- Coordinate readiness for adoption, training, or support enablement
- Help identify usability gaps and success criteria tied to user outcomes
- Support launch messaging and communication with customer-facing teams
- Share feedback loops from field experience back into planning and iteration

### Goals
- Improve adoption, satisfaction, and value realization
- Reduce friction during rollout and post-launch support
- Connect product delivery to customer outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Feedback review with product and engineering teams
- Launch readiness and enablement planning
- Customer-facing updates and adoption monitoring

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how the team measures success and uses data to improve the product, service, or delivery process. They help connect execution to outcomes and business value.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and measurement plans
- Support instrumentation and reporting requirements for feature adoption or operational health
- Review data quality and signal integrity before making decisions
- Help teams interpret performance trends and customer impact
- Partner with PMs and product leads to monitor outcomes after launch

### Goals
- Make decisions based on evidence instead of assumptions
- Improve visibility into product performance and operational health
- Connect delivery work to measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metric planning and dashboard reviews
- Outcome tracking with PM, product, and stakeholders
- Insights sharing during weekly updates and milestone reviews

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons help align sponsor, customer, and cross-functional stakeholders around priorities, decisions, and project status. They reduce communication gaps and ensure expectations are visible and understood.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate stakeholder updates and communication timing
- Surface decisions, risks, and trade-offs to the right audience
- Support issue escalation and escalation tracking across teams
- Help ensure stakeholder expectations remain aligned with delivery reality
- Facilitate collaboration across groups with different priorities

### Goals
- Improve transparency and stakeholder confidence
- Reduce misalignment and decision delays
- Keep cross-functional work moving with clear ownership

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Decision and risk briefings for sponsors and cross-functional leaders
- Escalation support and alignment meetings

---

## How these personas interact with existing roles

- Developers work closely with QA, Architecture, and Release Managers to deliver, validate, and ship features safely.
- Product Managers partner with Customer Success, Data Leads, and Stakeholder Liaisons to define outcomes, monitor success, and align stakeholder expectations.
- Project Managers coordinate across PM, QA, Release, Security, and stakeholder roles to maintain schedule, risk visibility, and execution rhythm.
- Security / Compliance Partners and Architecture / Technical Leads support engineering decisions and governance at design, review, and release stages.
- Customer Success, Data, and Stakeholder roles connect project execution to business outcomes, adoption, and communication quality.
- Each persona adds accountability in a different part of the project lifecycle, helping the team reduce ambiguity and improve decision quality.
